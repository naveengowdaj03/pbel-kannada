Welcome Residents

Celebrating Kannada State Together

Kannada Habba at PBEL City brings together all PBELites to honor the glorious heritage, language, arts, and warmth of Karnataka State. All PBEL residents are warmly welcome!

Creative Art Competition

Nostalgic Traditional Game

Lagori Championship

Relive the high-spirited traditional stone-stacking Indian game (Seven Stones)! Tower vs tower friendly matches packed with nostalgia.

PBEL City Sports Arena
