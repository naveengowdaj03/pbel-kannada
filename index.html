Resident Information

Audience Guide & FAQs

Everything you need to know about attending, participating, and enjoying the celebration.

Every single PBELite — across all towers and blocks — is warmly invited! Bring your kids, parents, and visiting grandparents. Whether you speak Kannada fluently or just love Karnataka State's culture, food, and music, you are welcome with open arms.

Traditional Indian and Karnataka State attire is enthusiastically encouraged! Yellow and Red (Karnataka State flag colours), Mysore silk sarees, kurtas, dhotis, and ethnic dress will make the atmosphere colourful and festive.

The Grand Community Lunch will be served on Sunday, 1st November from 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM at the Clubhouse Banquet Hall. To ensure enough authentic meals are freshly prepared for all families, please use the registration button below to reserve your family's attendance.

Use the registration button below the event you wish to join (Cooking Without Fire, Clay Art, Lagori, Cultural Performances, or Ramp Walk). Participation is completely free for all PBELites.

For the Clay Art Workshop, natural clay and shaping sticks will be provided on the spot. For Cooking Without Fire, participants should bring their own pre-chopped ingredients, serving plates, and non-electrical tools.
